04 — Key Projects

Where the real work happened.

A lot of the PBD role is relationship management, not just spreadsheets. These are the two projects that taught me that firsthand.

Signature Deliverable

The Partner Mapping Tool

What started as a simple classification exercise turned into a tool the team can use. Us interns organized every partner by track, expertise, and program, then reworked it twice based on feedback from colleagues.

WK 3 Partner Mapping V1: classified partners, built the consolidated master Excel.
WK 4 Shipped V2 with reseller pipeline visualization, plus the Go Cloud requirement.
WK 5 Normalized the data and built the dashboard and opportunities view.
WK 6-10 Adopted into live PBD workflows: Go Cloud, Track 3, reseller tracking.
The Project I Owned End-to-End

Cloud Team Building

I was handed the full project, from picking the venue to running it on the day. It's the closest I've come to managing something end-to-end.

PLAN Negotiated Canyon Woods & Canyon Cove venues against budget and expectations.
FUND In constant communication with Ada regarding the budget.
RUN Managed the master list, shuttle, games, and room assignments.
LIVE Handled on-the-ground execution, communicating with the organizers and making alternative solutions.
"My first customer as a BD wasn't a partner or a distributor — it was Ada, our HR Director, while I ran team building for the whole Cloud team."
— A moment worth noting
Every stakeholder countsAda had expectations, a budget, and a timeline to meet, which felt a lot like managing a partner account.
Not just externalMuch of what BD work really is showed up here first: coordination, follow-ups, and keeping everyone informed.
Habits transferStaying organized and communicating clearly with Ada is the same discipline I'll need when working with any partner.
"In Week 10, I didn't wait for an assignment. I reached out to the PBDs myself and asked to be in the room for the Stratpoint and WSI deals. Not to observe — to help."
— From my Week 10 reflection
I asked instead of waitingNobody assigned me to those meetings — I reached out to the PBDs directly and asked to be included.
It paid offThat one conversation is what got me into the Stratpoint and WSI deals, weeks earlier than I otherwise would have.
Worth repeatingIt's a small habit, but it's the one I want to carry into the next three months.
