04 — EXECUTIVE REFLECTIONS

Moments Worth Noting

Key leadership takeaways on stakeholder management and proactive deal initiative from the field.

"My first customer as a BD wasn't a partner or a distributor — it was Ada, our HR Director, while I ran team building for the whole Cloud team."
— A Moment Worth Noting: Internal Stakeholder Discipline
Every stakeholder counts
Ada had expectations, a budget, and a timeline to meet, which felt a lot like managing a partner account.
Not just external
Much of what BD work really is showed up here first: coordination, follow-ups, and keeping everyone informed.
Habits transfer
Staying organized and communicating clearly with Ada is the same discipline I’ll need when working with any partner.
"In Week 10, I didn't wait for an assignment. I reached out to the PBDs myself and asked to be in the room for the Stratpoint and WSI deals. Not to observe — to help."
— Another Moment Worth Noting: Proactive Initiative
I asked instead of waiting
Nobody assigned me to those meetings — I reached out to the PBDs directly and asked to be included.
It paid off
That one conversation is what got me into the Stratpoint and WSI deals, weeks earlier than I otherwise would have.
Worth repeating
It's a small habit, but it's the one I want to carry into the next three months.
